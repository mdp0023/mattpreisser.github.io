Hi, I’m Matthew Preisser, PhD

Postdoctoral Researcher
Institute for Environmental Engineering
ETH Zürich

I earned my PhD in Civil Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin, along with master's degrees in Public Affairs and Environmental & Water Resources Engineering. I am currently a postdoc at ETH Zürich, working under the Chair of Hydrology and Water Resources Management.

My research focus is in developing tools and methods to describe and predict the compounding impacts of hazards on society. I use remote sensing, conceptual modeling, dynamical systems, network analyses, and open source data/tools to address the intersection of hazards on vulnerable populations in near-real time from both engineering and social science viewpoints.

This research portfolio is under continuous maintenance, and will be updated as projects develop. Please explore this site for information about my ongoing and previous research, and do not hestitate to reach out to learn more or to discuss future collaborations. My contact information can be found on the menu bar to the left.

My Research

Socio-Hydrology

Studying of the interconnected relationships between people and water using a simplified conceptual model of society and the environment

Remote Sensing Multi-Hazards

Utilizing a high-resolution satellite remote sensing methodology to increase the detection of flooding and heat events

Near-Real-Time Compound Flood Mapping

Examining how fluvial and pluvial flooding impact vulnerable populations with a topographic based inundation methodology

Critical Resource Accessibility

Quantifying resiliency metrics for household level disruptions to critical resources during and immediately after flooding events
